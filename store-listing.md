## Store listing tab

**Name:** Readable Text (taken from the zip)

**Summary:**
Makes long text on ZenHR easy to read: keeps line breaks and shows bold text and bullet lists.

**Description:**
Long answers on ZenHR, like self-reflections and feedback, show up as one big block of text because line breaks are lost when they're displayed.

Readable Text fixes that:
• Keeps your line breaks and paragraphs
• Shows bold text when you write **like this**
• Turns lines starting with "- " into bullet lists

It only changes how text looks on your screen. Nothing is changed on ZenHR, and people without the extension still see the original text.

Privacy: everything runs locally in your browser. No data is collected, stored or sent anywhere.

Readable Text is an independent project and is not affiliated with ZenHR.
